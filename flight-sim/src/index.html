First flight: start on the runway, throttle to full, keep straight with Q/E, and gently pull back at rotation speed. On approach, follow the magenta ILS diamonds and the PAPI lights (2 white / 2 red = on glide path). Flare a few metres above the runway and aim for under 200 fpm at touchdown.

FLIGHT CONTROLLERS

Joysticks, sidesticks, yokes, throttle quadrants, rudder pedals (with toe brakes) and gamepads all work, and several can be used at once. Each device gets its own saved profile. Use Setup wizard to map your hardware by simply moving each control.
